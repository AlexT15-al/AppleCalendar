# Reference notes

Facts about Apple Calendar on iPhone (iOS 26/27) that the screenshots alone do
not show. Sources: Apple's iPhone User Guide for iOS 27 ("Change how you view
events in Calendar", "Create and edit events in Calendar"), supplied by the
project owner.

## Views and how they are reached

- **Month view** has a *Month view icon* (top right, the separate circle) whose
  menu offers **Compact** (a single bar indicator per day), **Stacked** (a bar
  indicator per event), **Details** (event titles) and **List** (tap each day
  to see its events listed).
- **Day view** has a *Day view icon* whose menu offers **Multi Day** and
  **List** (a complete list of upcoming events).
- **Week**: rotate the iPhone sideways in Month or Day view.
- iPhone Duo (landscape) has a view pop-up: Planner, List, Day, Week, Month,
  Year. Out of scope for now.

## Events

- New event: in Day view tap **+**. Title field; with Apple Intelligence a
  natural-language description fills the event card ("Soccer game on Saturday
  from 11 a.m. - 1 p.m.").
- Then **Location or Video Call** (FaceTime link), start and end, travel time,
  invitees, attachments. Confirm with the ✓.
- Alerts: tap the event → **Edit** → Alert → "At time of event", "5 minutes
  before", … A located event can alert when it is time to leave.
- Attachments: Edit → Add attachment; swipe left on one to Remove.
- Copy / paste: touch and hold an event → **Copy**; touch and hold a time on
  another date to paste (New Event opens with the copied event offered below
  the title).
- Move / resize: in Day or Week view, touch and hold an event and drag it, or
  drag its grab points.
- Delete: tap the event → **Delete Event**.
